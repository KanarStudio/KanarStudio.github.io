Privacy Policy — GlowDeck

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the GlowDeck application (hereinafter "the App") collects, processes, and protects user data. The App is developed by Kanar Studio (hereinafter "the Developer").

1. Data Controller

The data controller within the meaning of the GDPR is:

Kanar Studio
Email: wawrzenczyk.piotr@gmail.com

2. What Data the App Collects

2.1 Data stored exclusively on the device

The App stores the following data locally on your device (in the app's database and private file directory). This data is never sent to any server:

  • Notes — note text, colors, alarm settings
  • Photos — photos imported from the gallery, copied to the app's private directory (filesDir/photos/)
  • Calendar events — read from the system content provider (CalendarContract), used solely for display on the wallpaper. The App does not store a copy of events outside the device
  • Wallpaper settings — effect preferences, colors, widget layout
  • Alarm sounds — if you selected a custom alarm sound, its URI is stored locally

2.2 Data necessary for functionality

The App does not require account creation, does not collect email addresses, names, or surnames. It does not collect demographic data.

4. Where Data Is Stored

  • User data (notes, photos, settings, calendar) — exclusively on your device in the app's private directory. It is not transmitted to any external servers.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all user data from the device.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob in two forms:

  • Banner ads — an anchored adaptive banner displayed at the bottom of the settings screens and in the widget picker. It is hidden permanently after purchasing the paid version.
  • Rewarded ads — shown only when you explicitly tap "Watch ad" to unlock premium features for 30 minutes. The App never shows interstitial ads.

When ads are loaded or displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device or other IDs (advertising ID) — for ad serving and fraud prevention
  • Ad interactions — e.g., that an ad was viewed or completed
  • SDK diagnostics — crash and performance data of the ads component

These data are processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising terms.

Consent (EEA / GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the App asks for your consent via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad request is made. You can choose personalized or non-personalized ads, or decline — declining never blocks the App's core features (premium widgets can still be unlocked via the free first-time trial).

6. Sharing Data with Third Parties

The Developer does not share your personal data (notes, photos, settings) with any third parties — that data never leaves your device. The only third party receiving data is Google AdMob, limited to the advertising data described in section 5 and only in connection with displaying ads.

7. User Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — you can check what data is stored (all of it is on your device)
  • Right to erasure — uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data
  • Right to rectification — you can edit notes and settings within the App
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can revoke permissions in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → GlowDeck → Permissions) and reset or withdraw your ad consent via the Android ads settings (Settings → Google → Ads)

To exercise these rights, contact the Developer at the email address indicated in section 1.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us to have it deleted.

9. Security

User data is stored in the app's private directory, which is not accessible to other apps (since Android 4.4 — app sandbox). The App itself does not transmit your personal data over the network; communication related to ads is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted HTTPS connections.
